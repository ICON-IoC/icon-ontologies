ICON Ontology Ecosystem

The PPOD model, unpacked into one ontology per kind of record. Each module stands on its own, builds on existing OBO ontologies where they fit, and links to the others through the relations its records use. Select a module to see what it reuses and how it connects.

Existing ontologies each module uses

Every module names these in its header (dcterms:references) and in its guide. OBO ontologies form the backbone; the W3C and community vocabularies supply record attributes and the mappings used for exchange.

Reused: a term used as is, under its own ID Built on: a parent, domain or range of the module's own terms Mapped to: a SKOS match for exchange

How it was checked. Every module and the merged set pass the OBO Foundry's ROBOT report with no errors, warnings or notes, sit in the OWL 2 DL profile, and are consistent under the HermiT reasoner. Every reused term was copied from its source ontology, and every mapping target was confirmed to exist there.
