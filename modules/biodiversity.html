ICON Taxa and habitats ontology

The living things and habitats records concern: taxa (species and groups, identified by NatureServe) habitat types (California Wildlife Habitat Relationships), vegetation types (Manual of California Vegetation alliances under the US National Vegetation Classification) coastal and marine habitat types (CMECS) and the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology, the links between those classifications, and the links that say a record concerns a taxon or a habitat type, or that a place is habitat for a taxon.

Terms

Reused terms keep their own IDs and link to their source ontology.

TermKindIDDefinition
taxon class ICON:0011000 A species or a group of species that records concern, identified by its NatureServe element where it has one. Groups (salmonids, bats) are taxa at a higher rank or informal groupings of them.
CWHR habitat type class ICON:0011001 A habitat type of the California Wildlife Habitat Relationships system, the state's standard classification of wildlife habitats (59 types, such as Valley Foothill Riparian or Blue Oak Woodland), identified by its CWHR code.
vegetation type class ICON:0011004 A unit of vegetation: an alliance of the Manual of California Vegetation, as CDFW's California Natural Communities List names it (such as Valley oak woodland and forest, or Fremont's goldfields - Downingia vernal pools), or a group, macrogroup or division of the US National Vegetation Classification above it (#1409).
coastal and marine habitat type class ICON:0011005 A unit of the Coastal and Marine Ecological Classification Standard (CMECS, FGDC-STD-018-2012): a system, subsystem or tidal zone of its Aquatic Setting (such as Estuarine Coastal Intertidal Tidal Zone), or a biotic setting, class, subclass or group of its Biotic Component (such as Seagrass Bed), identified by its CMECS ID (#1410).
IUCN ecosystem type class ICON:0011006 A unit of the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology 2.0 (Keith et al. 2022), the global classification of ecosystems by how they function: one of its 11 realms (such as Terrestrial), 25 biomes (such as Savannas and grasslands biome) or 110 ecosystem functional groups (such as T4.4 Temperate woodlands), identified by its GET code and IRI (#1411).
invasive rating class ICON:0011002 How invasive a non-native species is in California: for plants, the California Invasive Plant Council (Cal-IPC) rating in its Inventory, High, Moderate, Limited, or Watch for a plant at high risk of becoming invasive (#410); for animals, listing by the California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW), which does not rate them (#435).
California Rare Plant Rank class ICON:0011003 The California Native Plant Society's rank of how rare a plant, bryophyte, or lichen is in California, from 1A (presumed extirpated or extinct) to 4 (a watch list of limited distribution), assigned with CDFW's California Natural Diversity Database and printed in its Special Vascular Plants, Bryophytes, and Lichens List. The decimal threat rank that follows it (.1 seriously, .2 moderately, .3 not very threatened) is kept as the link's source note (#1210).
concerns taxon relation ICON:0011100 The record is about, works for or affects the species or group.
is taxon of relation ICON:0011101 The reverse of concerns taxon.
has habitat type relation ICON:0011102 A CWHR habitat type (#169), vegetation type (#1409) or coastal and marine habitat type (#1410) the record covers, works in or affects.
is habitat type of relation ICON:0011103 The reverse of has habitat type.
has broader habitat type relation ICON:0011108 Links a habitat or vegetation type to a broader one that contains it: an MCV alliance to its USNVC group, a group to its macrogroup, a CMECS unit to the unit above it, a GET functional group to its biome and a biome to its realm, and an alliance or CMECS unit to the one CWHR habitat type it falls in. A record that has the narrower type has the broader one too (#1409, #1410, #1411).
has narrower habitat type relation ICON:0011109 The reverse of has broader habitat type.
is related to habitat type relation ICON:0011110 Links an MCV alliance or CMECS unit to each CWHR habitat type it may fall in, when there is more than one: a stand of it falls within one of them, but not always the same one. Nothing passes along it (#1409, #1410).
has IUCN ecosystem group relation ICON:0011111 Links a CWHR, MCV or CMECS habitat type to the IUCN GET ecosystem functional groups it falls in, or to the biome or realm when it spans several of their groups. Mapped by hand in reference/get-crosswalk.csv at the broadest unit where the match holds and stated on every unit below it. Nothing passes along it: records are not tagged with GET (#1411).
is IUCN ecosystem group of relation ICON:0011112 The reverse of has IUCN ecosystem group.
has invasive rating relation ICON:0011104 The taxon is invasive in California, or at risk of becoming so, with this Cal-IPC rating (#410) or CDFW listing (#435).
is invasive rating of relation ICON:0011105 The reverse of has invasive rating.
has rare plant rank relation ICON:0011106 The plant, bryophyte, or lichen has this California Rare Plant Rank in CDFW's Special Vascular Plants, Bryophytes, and Lichens List (#1210).
is rare plant rank of relation ICON:0011107 The reverse of has rare plant rank.
Scientific Name attribute dwc:scientificName The full scientific name, with authorship and date information if known. When forming part of a dwc:Identification, this should be the name in lowest level taxonomic rank that can be determined.
Vernacular Name attribute dwc:vernacularName A common or vernacular name.
Taxon ID attribute dwc:taxonID An identifier for a dwc:Taxon.
NatureServe global rank attribute ICON:0011200 The taxon's NatureServe conservation status across its whole range, G1 (critically imperiled) to G5 (secure), with T ranks for subspecies.
taxon kind attribute ICON:0011201 Whether the term is a single NatureServe taxon (taxon), a group of taxa (group), or matched nothing in NatureServe (none).
informal group attribute ICON:0011202 NatureServe's informal taxonomy path for the taxon, such as Animals | Vertebrates | Birds.
group level attribute ICON:0011203 For a group, what defines it: NatureServe's informal taxonomy, or a rank of its scientific taxonomy (kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, subfamily, genus).
group value attribute ICON:0011204 For a group, the name at its group level, such as Salmoninae for a subfamily group.
genera attribute ICON:0011205 For a subfamily group, which NatureServe cannot search by rank, the genera it spans, separated by semicolons: Oncorhynchus; Salmo; Salvelinus.
vegetation classification level attribute ICON:0011210 Where the vegetation type sits: an MCV Alliance, Semi-Natural Alliance (dominated by non-native plants) or Special Stands, or a USNVC Group, Macrogroup or Division.
CDFW CaCode attribute ICON:0011211 The alliance's code on CDFW's California Natural Communities List, such as 71.035.00 for Valley oak woodland and forest.
life form attribute ICON:0011212 The alliance's primary life form on the Natural Communities List: Tree, Shrub, Herb or Non-vascular.
natural community global rank attribute ICON:0011213 How rare and threatened the alliance is across its whole range, G1 (very rare and threatened) to G5 (secure), from NatureServe's method as CDFW applies it.
natural community state rank attribute ICON:0011214 How rare and threatened the alliance is in California, S1 to S5; SNA for a semi-natural alliance, which is not ranked.
sensitive natural community attribute ICON:0011215 Yes for an alliance with a state rank of S1 to S3, which CDFW counts as a sensitive natural community for environmental review under CEQA.
CMECS classification level attribute ICON:0011220 Where the CMECS unit sits: a System, Subsystem or Tidal Zone of the Aquatic Setting, or a Biotic Setting, Biotic Class, Biotic Subclass or Biotic Group of the Biotic Component.
CMECS code attribute ICON:0011221 The unit's code in the CMECS Catalog, its component's letter (A for the Aquatic Setting, B for the Biotic Component) followed by its unit code, such as B2.5.2.1 for Seagrass Bed.
CMECS ID attribute ICON:0011222 The unit's permanent identifier in the CMECS Catalog, such as CMECS_00000721; https://w3id.org/CMECS/ followed by it is the unit's IRI.
GET classification level attribute ICON:0011230 Where the IUCN GET unit sits: a Realm, a Biome or a Functional group (an ecosystem functional group).
GET code attribute ICON:0011231 The unit's code in the IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology: a realm's letters (T, or MT for a transitional realm), a biome's (T4) or a functional group's (T4.4).
GET IRI attribute ICON:0011232 The unit's permanent identifier in the IUCN GET SKOS vocabulary, such as https://w3id.org/iucn-get/EFG_T4_4.
definition attribute skos:definition A statement or formal explanation of the meaning of a concept.
high invasive rating value ICON:0011010 Cal-IPC: severe ecological impacts on physical processes, plant and animal communities and vegetation structure, with rates of dispersal and establishment that are moderate to high; most are widely distributed.
moderate invasive rating value ICON:0011011 Cal-IPC: substantial and apparent, but generally not severe, ecological impacts, with moderate to high rates of dispersal, though establishment generally depends on disturbance.
limited invasive rating value ICON:0011012 Cal-IPC: invasive, but with ecological impacts that are minor statewide, or too little information to rate it higher; often locally persistent and problematic.
watch invasive rating value ICON:0011013 Cal-IPC: not yet invasive in California's wildlands, but assessed as at high risk of becoming invasive here.
CDFW listed invasive rating value ICON:0011014 CDFW: an animal on the Invasive Species Program's list California's Invaders, as invasive in California or a threat to the state; CDFW does not rate how invasive it is (#435).
California Rare Plant Rank 1A value ICON:0011020 CNPS: Plants presumed extirpated in California and either rare or extinct elsewhere: not seen or collected in the wild in California for many years.
California Rare Plant Rank 1B value ICON:0011021 CNPS: Plants rare, threatened, or endangered in California and elsewhere; most are endemic to California and have declined significantly over the last century.
California Rare Plant Rank 2A value ICON:0011022 CNPS: Plants presumed extirpated in California but common elsewhere.
California Rare Plant Rank 2B value ICON:0011023 CNPS: Plants rare, threatened, or endangered in California but more common elsewhere.
California Rare Plant Rank 3 value ICON:0011024 CNPS: Review list: plants about which more information is needed, nearly all taxonomically problematic.
California Rare Plant Rank 4 value ICON:0011025 CNPS: Watch list: plants of limited distribution or infrequent throughout a broader area of California, whose status should be monitored.
