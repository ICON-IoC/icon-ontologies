ICON Core ontology

The shared top of the ICON ontologies: the broad kinds of record (agent, endeavor, place, information resource, planning element), the involvement and funding links every kind of record uses, and the annotation properties the other modules share. Every other module builds on it.

TermKindIDDefinition
agent class ICON:0000010 An organization or a person: something that can lead, fund, own, manage or take part in conservation work.
endeavor class ICON:0000011 A planned process carried out to achieve conservation ends over a span of time: a project or a program.
place class ICON:0000012 A part of the landscape or a structure in it that records can be located at, worked on or bounded by: a river, a reserve, a dam, a crossing structure.
information resource class ICON:0000013 An information content entity that records or publishes information: a data resource, a paper or a report.
planning element class ICON:0000014 A directive information entity that sets out what conservation work should achieve or do: a plan, one of its objectives, or one of its actions.
entity class BFO:0000001 The explorer's top class Resource, which every record belongs to.
Concept class skos:Concept An idea or notion; a unit of thought.
is involved with relation ICON:0000100 A link between two records that work together or affect one another. Every more specific involvement link (leads, funds, manages, uses, is partner of and others) implies it.
led by relation ICON:0000101 An organization or person that directs the work and answers for it.
leads relation ICON:0000102 An organization or person directs this work: the reverse of led by.
used by relation ICON:0000103 An organization, project or program relies on this place, data or tool in its work.
uses relation ICON:0000104 The record relies on this place or data in its work: the reverse of used by.
funded by relation ICON:0000105 An organization or funding source that paid for the work, the data or the publication.
funds relation ICON:0000106 The organization or funding source pays for this work, data or publication: the reverse of funded by and of funded through.
funded through relation ICON:0000107 A program or a guideline or mandate whose money paid for the work, such as a grant program or a bond measure. A kind of funded by, naming the channel the money came through.
was created by relation ICON:0000108 The organization or person that produced the record's subject, or the guideline or mandate that brought an organization into being.
created relation ICON:0000109 The organization, person or mandate produced this: the reverse of was created by.
informs relation ICON:0000110 The data or publication is drawn on by a plan, an objective, an action or a guideline or mandate, as survey counts inform a recovery objective.
is informed by relation ICON:0000111 The plan, objective, action or mandate draws on this data or publication: the reverse of informs.
