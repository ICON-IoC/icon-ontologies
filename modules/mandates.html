ICON Guidelines and mandates ontology

Laws, regulations, executive orders, agreements, certifications and guidelines that require, allow, fund or shape conservation work, and the ways organizations stand toward them: bound by, implementing, enforcing, issuing, certified by.

TermKindIDDefinition
guideline or mandate class ICON:0006000 A directive that requires, allows, funds or shapes conservation work: a law, regulation, executive order, agreement, certification standard or guideline. Some bind; others only advise.
agreement class ICON:0006001 A guideline or mandate that two or more parties sign, such as a memorandum of understanding, a settlement or a habitat conservation plan's implementing agreement.
certification standard class ICON:0006002 A guideline or mandate that sets the practices land, products or operations must meet to be certified, such as a sustainable farming or forestry standard.
executive order class ICON:0006003 A guideline or mandate that a president or governor issues to direct government agencies.
guideline class ICON:0006004 A guideline or mandate that recommends how something should be done without binding anyone.
legislation class ICON:0006005 A guideline or mandate that a legislature or the voters enact: a statute, an act, a ballot measure.
regulation class ICON:0006006 A guideline or mandate that an agency adopts under authority a statute gives it, with the force of law, as in the California Code of Regulations.
agency rule class ICON:0006007 A guideline or mandate that a body adopts to govern its own lands, programs or members, short of a codified regulation.
court order class ICON:0006008 A guideline or mandate that a court issues in a case, such as a writ of mandate or an injunction, directing a party, often a government agency, to act or to stop acting. It binds the parties named in it. Example: the 2025 writ of mandate in California Chaparral Institute v. Board of Forestry and Fire Protection, which limits the California Vegetation Treatment Program in chaparral and coastal sage scrub.
government level class ICON:0006010 The level of government a guideline or mandate comes from or applies at.
has instrument type relation ICON:0006100 Links a guideline or mandate to one of the instrument type classes it belongs to. The explorer's way of stating its class.
is instrument type of relation ICON:0006101 The reverse of has instrument type.
at government level relation ICON:0006102 The level of government a guideline or mandate comes from.
is government level of relation ICON:0006103 The reverse of at government level.
is required by relation ICON:0006104 A guideline or mandate requires this data, publication or plan to be produced, as the Endangered Species Act requires recovery plans.
requires relation ICON:0006105 The reverse of is required by.
is bound by relation ICON:0006106 A guideline or mandate binds the organization: it must comply with it. A project, program or other record is bound by a mandate only when a source says so for that record.
binds relation ICON:0006107 The reverse of is bound by.
implements relation ICON:0006108 An organization, project, program or guideline or mandate carries out a guideline or mandate or a plan, as a program carries out the statute that authorizes it or the certification it runs, or a regulation carries out the statute that requires it.
is implemented by relation ICON:0006109 The reverse of implements.
enforces relation ICON:0006110 An organization holds the authority to enforce a guideline or mandate.
is enforced by relation ICON:0006111 The reverse of enforces.
issued relation ICON:0006112 An organization issued a guideline, mandate or plan: adopted, signed or published it.
was issued by relation ICON:0006113 The reverse of issued.
is supported by relation ICON:0006114 An organization's work rests on a guideline or mandate, as a program on the statute that authorizes it.
supports relation ICON:0006115 The reverse of is supported by.
advises about relation ICON:0006116 An organization gives advice on how to meet or apply a guideline or mandate.
is advised about by relation ICON:0006117 The reverse of advises about.
is certified by relation ICON:0006118 An organization, or a place such as a ranch or preserve, holds certification under a certification standard.
certifies relation ICON:0006119 The reverse of is certified by.
funds established by relation ICON:0006120 An organization pays out money a guideline or mandate set up, as a board awarding bond-measure grants.
sponsored relation ICON:0006121 An organization sponsored a bill or ballot measure: it brought the proposal to the legislator who carried it, or campaigned for the measure, and backed it to passage.
was sponsored by relation ICON:0006122 The reverse of sponsored.
is authorized under relation ICON:0006123 A project went ahead under a permit, order, biological opinion or exemption that approved its work, such as a restoration project covered by a programmatic biological opinion or a nationwide permit.
authorizes relation ICON:0006124 The reverse of is authorized under.
federal level value ICON:0006011 The United States government.
state level value ICON:0006012 The State of California.
local level value ICON:0006013 Counties, cities and special districts.
private level value ICON:0006014 No government: a standard or agreement set by private parties.
international level value ICON:0006015 An agreement among national governments, such as a treaty or a decision of the parties to a convention. Example: the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (2022), adopted under the Convention on Biological Diversity.
