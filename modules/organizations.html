ICON Organizations ontology

Organizations in conservation: agencies, non-profits, tribes, companies, universities and partnerships, their types and activities, how they nest and join one another, and the links by which they publish, host, fund or partner in other work.

TermKindIDDefinition
organization class OBI:0000245 An organization is a continuant entity which can play roles, has members, and has a set of organization rules. Members of organizations are either organizations themselves or individual people. Members can bear specific organization member roles that are determined in the organization rules. The organization rules also determine how decisions are made on behalf of the organization by the organization members.
academic organization class ICON:0001001 A university, college or research institute, or a department of one.
commercial organization class ICON:0001002 A business run for profit, such as a consultancy, a utility company, a farm business or a developer.
federal government organization class ICON:0001003 An agency or office of the United States government, such as the Fish and Wildlife Service or the Bureau of Reclamation.
state government organization class ICON:0001004 An agency, department, board or commission of the State of California, such as the Department of Fish and Wildlife.
local government organization class ICON:0001005 A county, city, special district (water, resource conservation, flood control) or one of their departments.
joint powers authority class ICON:0001006 A public agency formed by two or more public agencies under California's Joint Exercise of Powers Act to carry out a shared purpose, such as a regional water or habitat authority.
tribal organization class ICON:0001007 A tribal government or an organization of one or more tribes or tribal members.
non-profit organization class ICON:0001008 A private organization that does not distribute profit, such as a land trust, a watershed group or a conservation society.
foundation class ICON:0001009 A private organization, usually endowed, that makes grants to others' work.
partnership class ICON:0001010 A standing collaboration of several organizations with a name of its own, such as a regional conservation partnership or a working group.
team class ICON:0001011 A unit inside an organization with a name of its own, such as a program office or a field crew.
international organization class ICON:0001012 An organization whose members are national governments, set up by a treaty or convention, such as a regional fisheries management commission.
city government organization class ICON:0001013 An incorporated city or town of California, or one of its departments, boards or commissions.
county government organization class ICON:0001014 A county of California, or one of its departments, boards or commissions, such as a county parks department or fish and game commission.
special district class ICON:0001015 A local agency formed under state law to provide one or a few services within set boundaries, such as a water, irrigation, resource conservation, park, flood control or sanitation district.
regional agency class ICON:0001016 A local agency that serves several cities or counties together, such as a council of governments or a regional transportation commission.
organizational activity class ICON:0001020 A kind of work an organization carries out, from the Terms sheet's organization activities. An organization is linked to the kinds it is capable of.
acquisition activity class ICON:0001021 Buying land, water or easements, or rights in them, for conservation.
advising activity class ICON:0001022 Giving expert advice to landowners, agencies or other organizations.
advocacy activity class ICON:0001023 Arguing for a cause or a policy before the public or decision makers.
agricultural production activity class ICON:0001024 Growing crops or raising livestock.
certification activity class ICON:0001025 Checking that land, products or practices meet a standard and certifying them.
construction activity class ICON:0001026 Building or rebuilding structures, such as fish passage, levees or crossings.
coordinating activity class ICON:0001027 Bringing organizations together and keeping their work aligned.
education activity class ICON:0001028 Teaching the public, students or practitioners.
funding activity class ICON:0001029 Paying for other organizations' work, as through grants or contracts.
governing activity class ICON:0001030 Exercising public authority over a jurisdiction.
habitat enhancement activity class ICON:0001031 Restoring or improving habitat, such as replanting, removing invasive plants or reconnecting floodplains.
management activity class ICON:0001032 Managing land, water or wildlife over time.
planning activity class ICON:0001033 Preparing plans for conservation, land use or resources.
policy activity class ICON:0001034 Making or shaping policy.
production activity class ICON:0001035 Producing goods or energy other than crops and livestock.
regulating activity class ICON:0001036 Issuing and enforcing permits and rules.
research activity class ICON:0001037 Studying nature or conservation.
services activity class ICON:0001038 Providing services to others, such as technical, legal or financial services.
monitoring activity class ICON:0001039 Repeating surveys or measurements over years to track the status of species, habitats, water or fisheries.
has org type relation ICON:0001100 Links an organization to one of the organization type classes it belongs to. The explorer's way of stating the organization's class.
is org type of relation ICON:0001101 The reverse of has org type.
capable of relation RO:0002215 A relation between a material entity (such as a cell) and a process, in which the material entity has the ability to carry out the process.
is activity of relation ICON:0001102 A kind of activity an organization carries out: the reverse of capable of.
is member of relation ICON:0001103 An organization belongs to a coalition, association or partnership as a member while keeping its own identity. A kind of part of in the explorer.
has member relation ICON:0001104 The reverse of is member of.
has partner relation ICON:0001105 An organization that works on a project or program alongside its lead, without leading or funding it.
is partner of relation ICON:0001106 The reverse of has partner.
is published by relation ICON:0001107 The organization responsible for making data or a publication available, when it is not the creator.
published relation ICON:0001108 The reverse of is published by.
located in county relation ICON:0001109 The county that holds the organization's headquarters, main office or seat. An organization is located in at most one county, and may operate in many (#949).
is county location of relation ICON:0001110 The reverse of located in county: the county holds the organization's headquarters.
located in ecoregion relation ICON:0001111 The ecoregion section that holds the organization's headquarters, main office or seat. An organization is located in at most one ecoregion section, and may work in many (#949).
is ecoregion location of relation ICON:0001112 The reverse of located in ecoregion: the ecoregion section holds the organization's headquarters.
