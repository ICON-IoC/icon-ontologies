ICON Indicators, targets and outcomes ontology

What conservation work is measured by: indicators with their units, the targets that objectives, plans, programs and guidelines or mandates set on them, and the outcomes projects and places deliver, linked by the results chain (contributes to) so progress toward a target can be added up.

TermKindIDDefinition
indicator class ICON:0014000 Something measurable that shows progress toward a conservation goal, with the unit it is counted in, such as acres of habitat restored or miles of stream opened to fish passage. Targets and outcomes on the same indicator can be added up and compared.
target class ICON:0014001 The amount of an indicator that an objective, plan, program or guideline or mandate sets out to reach, by a year where it gives one: restore 5,000 to 7,000 acres of tidal marsh within 30 years. A range keeps its low and high values; a milestone is another target on the same indicator with an earlier year.
outcome class ICON:0014002 An amount of an indicator that a project, program or place delivers: expected, as a grant award or project plan states it, or measured, as a report or an inventory such as CPAD records it.
expected outcome class ICON:0014003 An outcome a project or program sets out to deliver and has not yet been shown to: the acres a grant award or a project plan states, or the planned and in-progress work a project tracker lists.
measured outcome class ICON:0014004 An outcome recorded as delivered, by a report, a monitoring survey or an inventory such as CPAD, or by a project tracker that lists the work as completed.
is measured by relation ICON:0014100 A record sets a target on the indicator, or reports an outcome on it.
measures relation ICON:0014101 The reverse of is measured by.
contributes to relation ICON:0014102 A project, program or place advances an objective, action, plan, program or guideline or mandate, so its outcomes count toward that record's targets.
is advanced by relation ICON:0014103 The reverse of contributes to.
has outcome relation ICON:0014104 A project, program or place delivers the outcome, expected or measured (#959). The explorer shows a record's outcomes in its panel rather than as links.
is outcome of relation ICON:0014105 The reverse of has outcome.
has target relation ICON:0014106 An objective, action, plan, program or guideline or mandate sets the target (#959). The explorer shows a record's targets, with progress toward them, in its panel rather than as links.
is target of relation ICON:0014107 The reverse of has target.
target low value attribute ICON:0014200 The target's amount in its indicator's unit, or the low end when the target is a range.
target high value attribute ICON:0014201 The high end of a target given as a range.
baseline value attribute ICON:0014202 The amount of the indicator before the target was set. Progress counts up from it: the baseline plus the outcomes achieved since.
baseline year attribute ICON:0014203 The year of the baseline value.
target year attribute ICON:0014204 The year by which the target is to be met.
outcome value attribute ICON:0014205 The outcome's amount in its indicator's unit.
outcome year attribute ICON:0014206 The year the outcome was measured, or is expected to be delivered.
target total attribute ICON:0014208 The whole a target's percentages are of, in its indicator's unit, such as California's land area for a goal of conserving 30 percent of its lands. A target with a total is shown in both amounts and percentages.
outcome status attribute ICON:0014207 Expected (an expected outcome, as a grant award or proposal states it) or measured (a measured outcome, as a report or inventory records it).
area conserved value ICON:0014010 Acres of land or water protected for conservation in fee title or by easement.
habitat restored value ICON:0014011 Acres where a habitat lost or degraded was re-established, such as tidal marsh restored from diked land.
habitat enhanced value ICON:0014012 Acres of existing habitat whose condition was improved without changing its type.
area under conservation management value ICON:0014013 Acres managed under a plan that benefits habitat or species, such as managed wetlands or grazing under a conservation plan.
floodplain reconnected value ICON:0014014 Acres of floodplain reconnected to its river, so that it floods again.
area treated for invasive species value ICON:0014015 Acres where invasive plants or animals were removed or controlled.
area treated for wildfire resilience value ICON:0014016 Acres of fuel reduction, prescribed fire or forest thinning.
area opened to public access value ICON:0014017 Acres newly opened to the public for recreation or access to nature.
stream habitat restored value ICON:0014018 Miles of stream channel or bank restored.
stream opened to fish passage value ICON:0014019 Miles of stream made reachable by migratory fish by removing or fixing barriers.
levee improved value ICON:0014020 Miles of levee repaired, raised or set back.
trail built or opened value ICON:0014021 Miles of trail built or opened to the public.
water for the environment value ICON:0014022 Acre-feet a year of water dedicated to streams, wetlands or habitat.
groundwater recharged value ICON:0014023 Acre-feet a year of water added to groundwater by recharge projects.
fish passage barriers remediated value ICON:0014024 The number of dams, culverts or other barriers to fish removed or fitted with passage.
wildlife crossings built value ICON:0014025 The number of wildlife crossing structures built or retrofitted.
populations secured value ICON:0014026 The number of populations of a species that meet a plan's test of being secure, such as protected and self-sustaining.
population size value ICON:0014027 The number of individuals of a species counted or estimated, such as adult fish returning to spawn.
projects completed value ICON:0014028 The number of projects of a kind carried out to completion.
partnerships formed value ICON:0014029 The number of partnerships or agreements formed among organizations.
funding invested value ICON:0014030 Dollars spent or awarded on the work.
carbon stored value ICON:0014031 Metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent stored or kept from release.
projects funded value ICON:0014032 The number of projects of a kind a funder approves a grant or acquisition for, counted when the award is made, whether or not the work is done yet.
projects monitored value ICON:0014033 The number of completed projects checked in a year for whether they are operated, used and maintained as their grant agreements require.
outreach events held value ICON:0014034 The number of meetings, workshops or conferences held or sponsored to reach the public, partners or prospective grantees.
process improvements made value ICON:0014035 The number of changes put in place to make an organization's processes, such as grant applications, reporting or payment, simpler or faster.
land conserved value ICON:0014036 Acres of land durably protected and managed for biodiversity, as California counts its 30x30 Conservation Areas on land. Narrower than area conserved, which takes in water and any protection by fee or easement.
coastal waters conserved value ICON:0014037 Acres of coastal waters durably protected and managed for biodiversity, as California counts its 30x30 Conservation Areas in its coastal state waters.
habitat created value ICON:0014038 Acres where a habitat was established where it did not exist before, such as a wetland built on upland. Habitat restored is where it once was.
