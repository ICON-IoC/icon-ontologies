ICON People ontology

People named in the graph: whom they are affiliated with, whom they are the contact for, and the leadership roles they hold. Names are public; contact details are kept out of the graph's public face and out of this ontology.

TermKindIDDefinition
Homo sapiens class NCBITaxon:9606 Reused from the NCBI taxonomy, as OBO ontologies do for a person: the explorer's Person class. Only names are public; email and phone are never served.
leadership role class ICON:0002001 A role in human social processes that a person holds when an organization, project or program puts them in charge of some part of it.
chief executive role class ICON:0002002 Heads the record's staff or operations: executive director, CEO, staff president, general manager, agency director or secretary, city manager, and the manager of a field office, region, refuge or preserve that is itself the record.
deputy executive role class ICON:0002003 Second in the staff line: deputy, associate or assistant director, chief operating officer, undersecretary or deputy secretary, assistant or associate field manager.
board chair role class ICON:0002004 Chairs the governing board, council or commission: chair, chairman, board president, tribal chair.
board officer role class ICON:0002005 An officer of the governing board other than its chair: vice chair, vice president of the board, secretary, treasurer.
program leader role class ICON:0002006 Leads a program, division or department within the record: program director, manager or coordinator, branch chief, program area chair.
project leader role class ICON:0002007 Leads a particular project: project manager, project coordinator, project lead.
science leader role class ICON:0002008 Leads the record's science: principal investigator, lead or chief scientist, science director.
elected official role class ICON:0002009 Holds an office the voters of a district or the state elect to: Assemblymember, State Senator, U.S. Representative, U.S. Senator, county supervisor.
is affiliated with relation ICON:0002100 A person works for, serves on the board of, or is otherwise attached to an organization. The link may carry the person's title and its role type.
has affiliate relation ICON:0002101 The reverse of is affiliated with.
has contact relation ICON:0002102 The person to ask about an organization, project, place or data resource. The person's contact details are private.
is contact for relation ICON:0002103 The reverse of has contact.
has expertise in relation ICON:0002104 A person knows an issue, a place, a taxon or a habitat type through their work: the projects, programs and plans they lead and the papers and reports they wrote address it, or the source records say so. Expertise is never passed down from the organizations a person belongs to.
is expertise of relation ICON:0002105 The reverse of has expertise in.
represents relation ICON:0002106 An elected official holds the seat of the district: a member of the State Assembly or State Senate, a member of Congress, or a county supervisor. A U.S. senator represents the whole state (#1432).
is represented by relation ICON:0002107 The reverse of represents.
role title attribute ICON:0002200 A person's title in the role a link records, as the organization or project writes it: Executive Director, Principal Investigator.
role type attribute ICON:0002201 The uniform kind of a role a link records, one of the leadership roles below, so titles that differ in wording can be compared.
LinkedIn profile attribute ICON:0002300 The address of the person's public LinkedIn profile: https://www.linkedin.com/in/name.
ORCID iD attribute ICON:0002301 The person's ORCID identifier, the persistent researcher id used by journals and funders: 0000-0002-1825-0097.
