ICON Places ontology

The areas records are placed in: California counties, USDA ecoregion sections, the HUC8 subbasins and HUC10 watersheds of the USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset, and the State Assembly, State Senate, congressional, and county supervisorial districts that elected officials represent, with statewide scope, and the links that say a record operates, applies or lies in one of them.

Terms

Reused terms keep their own IDs and link to their source ontology.

TermKindIDDefinition
county class ICON:0010000 One of California's 58 counties, as an administrative region rather than as its government.
ecoregion class ENVO:01000276 A large unit of land or water containing a geographically distinct assemblage of species, natural communities, and environmental conditions.
HUC8 subbasin class ICON:0010001 A drainage basin at the subbasin level (eight-digit hydrologic unit code) of the USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset.
HUC10 watershed class ICON:0010002 A drainage basin at the watershed level (ten-digit hydrologic unit code) of the USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset. Each lies within one HUC8 subbasin.
electoral district class ICON:0010005 An area whose voters elect an official to a legislature or a county board: a California State Assembly, State Senate, congressional or county supervisorial district (#1432).
State Assembly district class ICON:0010006 One of the 80 districts that each elect a member of the California State Assembly, as the Citizens Redistricting Commission drew them in 2021.
State Senate district class ICON:0010007 One of the 40 districts that each elect a California State Senator, as the Citizens Redistricting Commission drew them in 2021.
congressional district class ICON:0010008 One of California's 52 districts that each elect a member of the U.S. House of Representatives, as the map in force draws them: the 2021 Citizens Redistricting Commission map until January 3, 2027, then the Proposition 50 (AB 604) map.
supervisorial district class ICON:0010009 A district of a California county that elects one member of the county's board of supervisors, as the county's own current map draws it.
geographic scope class ICON:0010003 An extent a record can claim in place of a list of areas.
operates in relation ICON:0010100 The record works in, covers or is about the area. A county cell of All in a workbook links the record to statewide instead.
is place of work of relation ICON:0010101 The reverse of operates in.
applies in relation ICON:0010102 A guideline, mandate or plan has force or scope in the area.
is jurisdiction of relation ICON:0010103 The reverse of applies in.
located in area relation ICON:0010104 A place, objective or action lies in the area. A kind of operates in, and a kind of the Relation Ontology's located in.
is location of relation ICON:0010105 The reverse of located in area.
covers relation ICON:0010106 The record's area takes in the area. Listed with the place links since the explorer's phrase table began (#35); no link uses it at present.
is covered by relation ICON:0010107 The reverse of covers.
in ecoregion relation ICON:0010108 The record works in, covers or lies in the ecoregion section.
is ecoregion of relation ICON:0010109 The reverse of in ecoregion.
in subbasin relation ICON:0010110 The record works in, covers or lies in the HUC8 subbasin.
is subbasin of relation ICON:0010111 The reverse of in subbasin.
in watershed relation ICON:0010112 The record works in, covers or lies in the HUC10 watershed.
is watershed of relation ICON:0010113 The reverse of in watershed.
statewide value ICON:0010004 The whole of California. A record that operates, applies or lies statewide is linked to this value rather than to every county, ecoregion, subbasin and watershed (#281).
