ICON Papers and reports ontology

Peer-reviewed papers and technical reports: authors, publishers and sponsors, what a work describes, which data it uses, which works it cites, and where its study was done.

TermKindIDDefinition
paper or report class ICON:0009000 A written work that reports research, monitoring or assessment: a peer-reviewed paper or a technical report. Shown in the explorer as Papers & reports.
peer-reviewed paper class ICON:0009001 An article that independent experts reviewed before a journal or conference published it. A work counts as one only if its journal certainly peer-reviewed it.
technical report class ICON:0009002 A report an agency, organization or research group issues itself on a study, a monitoring program, an assessment or a project's results, usually in a numbered series. It may or may not have been reviewed.
publication availability class ICON:0009010 Who can read a paper or report. Values follow the open-access status Unpaywall and Crossref report.
has publication type relation ICON:0009100 Links a paper or report to the class it belongs to: peer-reviewed paper or technical report. The explorer's way of stating its class.
is publication type of relation ICON:0009101 The reverse of has publication type.
authored by relation ICON:0009102 A person or organization that wrote the work. The full author list as printed is the authors attribute, since most authors have no record of their own.
authored relation ICON:0009103 The reverse of authored by.
commissioned by relation ICON:0009104 The organization a technical report was prepared for, when someone else wrote it, as a consultant's report for an agency.
commissioned relation ICON:0009105 The reverse of commissioned by.
describes relation ICON:0009106 The work is about this project, program, organization or data resource: it reports, evaluates or documents it. A data paper describes its dataset.
is described by relation ICON:0009107 The reverse of describes.
uses data from relation ICON:0009108 The work's analysis, or the tool, draws on this dataset, database or repository. For a tool, the workbook's inDataset column.
provides data for relation ICON:0009109 The reverse of uses data from.
cites relation ICON:0009110 The work cites another paper or report in the graph.
is cited by relation ICON:0009111 The reverse of cites.
studied at relation ICON:0009112 A place where the work's field study or monitoring was done, such as a reserve, a creek or a dam.
is study site of relation ICON:0009113 The reverse of studied at.
authors attribute ICON:0009200 The author list as printed on the work: Smith, J., Lee, K. and Ortiz, M. Public bibliographic information, not contact details.
published in attribute ICON:0009201 The journal a paper appeared in, or the series a report belongs to, by name: Conservation Biology, USGS Open-File Report. Kept as text: journals are not records in the graph.
report number attribute ICON:0009202 A report's number in its series, such as 2019-1123.

issue attribute bibo:issue
